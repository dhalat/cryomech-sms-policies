Privacy Policy

David Halat CryoMech Alerts

Last updated: September 2026

Purpose

David Halat CryoMech Alerts is a small operational monitoring service that sends SMS notifications to laboratory colleagues who have explicitly agreed to receive alerts concerning a CryoMech helium system.

Information collected

The service may maintain a recipient's mobile telephone number and record their consent to receive CryoMech alert messages. Messaging providers may also generate technical information associated with message transmission, delivery, or opt-out requests.

How information is used

Mobile telephone numbers and associated consent information are used only to deliver requested CryoMech operational alerts, administer the messaging service, provide support, and honor opt-out requests.

Sharing of mobile information

Mobile information and SMS opt-in data and consent will not be shared, sold, rented, or provided to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Service providers involved in delivering the messaging service, such as telecommunications and messaging infrastructure providers, may process information only as necessary to provide the service.

Marketing

David Halat CryoMech Alerts does not send advertising or promotional messages.

Opting out

Recipients may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. Recipients may reply HELP for assistance.
