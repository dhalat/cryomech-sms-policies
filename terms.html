SMS alert program

David Halat CryoMech Alerts provides automated operational SMS notifications concerning a monitored CryoMech helium system. Messages may be generated when defined equipment conditions occur, such as compressor shutdown or monitored temperature or pressure alarm conditions.

The service is intended for a small group of laboratory colleagues who have explicitly agreed to receive these alerts.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies because messages are generated in response to equipment conditions. Periods with no alarms may result in no messages, while equipment alarm conditions may result in additional notifications.

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out

Reply STOP to any message to opt out of further SMS messages.

After an opt-out request is processed, no further CryoMech alert messages will be sent to that mobile number unless the recipient subsequently provides new consent.
